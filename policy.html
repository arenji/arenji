Policy Writing

Beyond academic research, I write policy analysis and research communication aimed at Norwegian policymakers and the public debate. This page collects my op-eds in Dagens Næringsliv, my posts on Norges Bank's Bankplassen blog, and my Norges Bank Staff Memos covering bank capital regulation, mortgage and household credit regulation, monetary policy transmission, negative interest rates, covered bonds, and household savings behavior. Most were written during my years at Norges Bank Research (2018–2025). Norwegian titles are accompanied by an English translation.
