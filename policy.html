Policy Research and Writing

This page collects research and writing from my policy work, most of it from my years at Norges Bank Research (2018–2025): directed research projects that were published in academic journals, op-eds in Dagens Næringsliv, posts on Norges Bank's Bankplassen blog, and Norges Bank Staff Memos.
